TRACE — Traceable Reasoning & Compliance Engine

Built an enterprise AI system for document intelligence, compliance analysis, explainable risk assessment, and evidence-backed reasoning during the 24-hour IGGNITRON Hackathon at KPR Institute of Technology (Secured 1st Prize).
